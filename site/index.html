In plain words, and who's who
In plain words (for non-sales readers): In Indian B2B sales, a salesperson has 100+ shops to call, promises a shop an extra discount on a phone call to win an order, and weeks later the shop pays less than the bill. Ten Today solves both problems: it picks the rep's top 10 actions for today, and makes sure every discount is approved by the right manager and locked with a digital seal before Finance ever sees the bill.

Benchmarks in detail
100+ → 10 / day Rep Daily Triage Cap Salesforce's State of Sales has repeatedly put time actually spent selling at well under a third of the week (Salesforce State of Sales). Capping Today at 10 rule-ranked actions, after the 1-3-5 rule, removes cherry-picking.
45% stall risk → 1d SLA Discount Approval Escalation 45% of companies have lost a deal in the past six months to slow quote approval (Conga research). Asking freezes the rep's task and auto-escalates from Kavita to Arjun after 1 working day.
2-person scheme & cap lock Margin & Scheme Governance Trade-scheme leakage is the quiet one: a rate agreed on a call, applied after the invoice, with nobody's second signature. Ten Today enforces role caps (≤10% Manager / >10% Head) and needs two approvers to activate a slab scheme, so one person can't create one retroactively. Design choice, not a cited statistic.
70-task slip → 0 fake "on-time" Anti-Gaming & Every Rupee R7 Todoist found users reschedule up to 70 overdue tasks day after day (MacStories). Ten Today measures on-time against the original target and blocks reps with >15d open Every Rupee claims.

Core BA Specification · Ranking, Lifecycle, RACI & Traceability

4-Rule Explicit Sort Order
  1. Pinned callbacks at customer's agreed time
  2. Priority tier: P1 (5+d late) → P2 (1–4d) → P3 (today)
  3. Money-blocking stage: Quote sent & Negotiation first
  4. Days overdue, then Deal value (tie-break only)
Stage-Driven Lifecycle

New lead (1d) → Qualified (quote) → Quote sent (3d) → Negotiation → Order placed.
Moving a stage auto-closes the old task and spawns the next step exactly once.

2-Way Every Rupee Bridge

Approved discounts carry a contractSeal (SHA-256) to Every Rupee. Unapproved short-payments (>15d) return as a rep recovery task that blocks new discount asks (Rule R7).
