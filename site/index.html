    Product calls

    What I didn't build, and why

    1. No AI deciding the discount. The rules decide and the numbers are shown; an approver can follow every one. AI only explains.
    2. No clawback from anyone's pay. Taking back pay already earned runs into wage-law trouble. Pay is earned on cash received, so there is nothing to claw back.
    3. No blocking the buyer's next order. Whether the buyer accepts a credit note is between them and finance. Ten Today raises a ticket; it doesn't hold the business hostage.
    4. No GST filing. Nothing is sent to the portal. Finance records the buyer's IMS action, so the tool never claims a status it can't see.

    Approval desk: ranking, lifecycle, RACI and traceability

    4-Rule Explicit Sort Order
    1. Pinned callbacks at customer's agreed time
    2. Priority tier: P1 (5+d late) → P2 (1–4d) → P3 (today)
    3. Money-blocking stage: Quote sent & Negotiation first
    4. Days overdue, then Deal value (tie-break only)
    Stage-Driven Lifecycle

    New lead (1d) → Qualified (quote) → Quote sent (3d) → Negotiation → Order placed.
    Moving a stage auto-closes the old task and spawns the next step exactly once.

    Sources: the rules, then the benchmarks

    The rules the controls follow
    • Post-sale discounts, prior agreement removed from 1 Nov 2026 · Finance Act 2026, Notification 03/2026-CT (TaxGuru)
    • s.34(2) deadline: 30 Nov or the annual return date, whichever is earlier (CA Sahuja)
    • IMS and credit notes: accept, reject, pending for one tax period (ClearTax) · what a rejection does to the supplier (Taxilla)
    • Financial credit notes and promotion services: CBIC Circular 251/08/2025 (Business Standard)
    • What's still unsettled: proof of reversal, classification, audit evidence (Nexdigm)
    • 72% of US trade promotions lose money, citing McKinsey (Tellius) · HUL FY25 gross margin 51.15%, used as an illustrative margin

    Benchmarks from the approval desk

    Benchmark / Control Fact Source How Ten Today Uses It
    Sales reps spend ~70% of their time on non-selling tasks (admin, list triage, internal follow-ups). Salesforce State of Sales Report Replaces 100+ open CRM rows with a 10-task rule-ranked daily queue.
    45% of B2B companies lost a deal in the prior 6 months due to slow quote/discount approvals. Conga Sales Cycle Handoff Survey (vendor benchmark) Freezes the rep's task on ask and auto-escalates undecided asks from Manager to Sales Head after 1 working day.
    Unauthorized trade schemes, missing maker-checker on slab masters, and post-facto rate changes drive FMCG margin leakage. Design choice — not a cited benchmark. Trade-scheme leakage is a known FMCG margin issue; the control is stated as a rule, not a statistic. Requires 2-person Maker-Checker to activate any slab scheme and seals every approval in a SHA-256 chain.
    Open-ended task lists cause overwhelm; users reschedule up to 70 overdue tasks day after day. Todoist 1-3-5 Method & MacStories Caps Today at 10, requires a blocker on the 2nd reschedule, and measures on-time against the original target date.

    What this does not claim: Ten Today is an independent product & BA specification with synthetic dealer data, not a deployed enterprise CRM. Public benchmarks above justify the control design; they are not measurements from a live customer deployment.
