1 · Business requirement document (one page)

Every rupee given back after a sale is checked before and after it leaves

Problem. Brands give money back to retailers after a sale: price cuts, promotion payouts, early-payment discounts, damage claims. Today it moves on email and spreadsheets. Nobody checks whether it paid for itself, whether the tax cut is valid, whether the retailer reversed its input tax, or whether the cash matches.

Why now. From 1 Nov 2026 the GST rules for post-sale discounts changed (Notification 03/2026-CT). A credit note for FY 2025-26 must be issued by the earlier of 30 Nov 2026 or the GSTR-9 filing date, or the tax cut is lost.

Who it hurts. Finance (money out without proof), tax (output tax cut that later gets reversed), receivables (short payments nobody can explain), auditors (no trail).

Where it sits. ① Today desk (before the sale: rep to-dos, price discount approved by the sales manager) → ② Discount controls (after the sale: rebates and credit notes approved by finance, seven checks) → ③ Every Rupee (cash matched). This pack covers ②.

In scope. Request, approval, classification, tax route, recovery through IMS, cash match, sealed evidence, weekly sign-off, MIS.

Out of scope. Filing returns, e-invoice generation, live ERP or GSTN connection, pricing strategy.

Success metric (north star). Rupees recovered or justified per ₹1 of discount given. Sample desk: ₹0.85. Guardrail: zero discounts approved by the person who asked for them.

Assumptions. Sample margin 51.15% (Hindustan Unilever's FY25 gross margin, StockAnalysis — a stand-in, not a fact about any retailer) and GST 18%. Owners are sample roles. A thinner margin makes a discount costlier: 6% off needs 13.3% more buying at 51.15%, 42.9% at 20%.

2 · Process map

As-is vs to-be

As-is 5 gaps

  1. SalesAgrees a % off with the retailer on a call
  2. SalesEmails finance. No break-even, no reason recorded
  3. FinanceApproves on trust; sometimes the requester approves
  4. AccountsIssues a GST credit note for everything, ads and damage included
  5. RetailerIgnores or rejects in IMS. Nobody follows up
  6. ReceivablesShort payment arrives. Nobody can tie it back

To-be with Ten Today 7 controls

  1. SalesEnters invoice + % + reason; break-even fills in C1
  2. FinanceDifferent person approves, within limit C2
  3. TaxDiscount vs service vs return decided C3
  4. TaxRight credit note, before the deadline C4
  5. TaxRetailer's IMS response tracked; reject → ticket C5
  6. ReceivablesPayment matched to approved net C6
  7. AuditEvery step sealed; weekly sign-off C7
